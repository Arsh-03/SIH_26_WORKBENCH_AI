# ASME Section VIII Division 1 - Pressure Vessel Inspection Standard

## 1. Scope & Design Rules
This specification establishes rules for pressure vessel construction, wall thickness calculation, and non-destructive testing (NDT) procedures.

## 2. Wall Thickness Calculation Formula
Minimum required circumferential shell thickness:
$$t = \frac{P \cdot R}{S \cdot E - 0.6 \cdot P}$$

Where:
- $P$ = Internal design pressure (psi or bar)
- $R$ = Inside radius of shell (inches or mm)
- $S$ = Maximum allowable stress value of material (psi or MPa)
- $E$ = Joint efficiency factor (1.00 for 100% radiographed welds)

## 3. Mandatory Inspection Intervals
- Hydrostatic pressure test at $1.5 \times$ design pressure prior to commissioning.
- Ultrasonic thickness gauging (UTG) required every 12 months for corrosive service vessels.
