# SOP-401: Industrial Boiler High-Temperature Operation Procedure

## 1. Objective & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) governs high-pressure steam boiler operations across sovereign industrial facilities. Compliance is mandatory for all licensed station engineers.

## 2. Operational Thresholds & Thermal Limits
- **Maximum Allowable Working Pressure (MAWP)**: 160.0 bar at baseline 350°C.
- **Thermal Degradation Rate**: Effective MAWP decreases linearly at higher temperatures according to formula:
  $$P_{\text{eff}} = 160.0 \times (1.0 - 0.0015 \times (T - 350))$$
- **High-Temperature Threshold**: At 500°C, the effective MAWP drops to 124.0 bar.

## 3. Pressure Relief Valve (PRV) Reset Calibration
- When operating above 480°C, safety relief valves MUST be recalibrated to 120.0 bar to guarantee a safety margin prior to initiating high-temperature feed lines.
- Relief valve inspection must be logged in the cryptographic enclave audit ledger every 24 operating hours.
